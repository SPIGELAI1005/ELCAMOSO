# Classic Vespa

**Profile ID:** `classic-vespa`

A stock PX 125-class scooter heard from beside the machine: one single-cylinder two-stroke pulse per crank revolution, a compact muffler, and a little mechanical texture. It does not share Traby's twin, crackle, or recorded loops.

Pulse rate is `rpm / 60`: about 31.7 Hz at 1,900 rpm and 93.3 Hz at 5,600 rpm. The worklet keeps that phase continuous. The body sits around 280-430 Hz and the edge around 1,050-1,400 Hz. Intake noise grows with load. There are no lift pops.

Idle is about 1,900 rpm. 5,600 rpm is the power-peak reference, not a rev limit. The soft ceiling is 7,800 rpm.

The four gears are approximations, not a verified Euro 3 tooth chart. Primary is the classic 22/67 (3.045) and the wheel is a 3.50-10 class circumference of 1.36 m. Gearbox ratios 4.83, 3.36, 2.57 and 2.00 put 5,600 rpm near 31, 45, 58 and 75 km/h. Shift hysteresis and a 1.2 s minimum dwell keep a steady cruise from hunting.
