RUMOSO coach voices
===================

Lines: "Push for it", "Go for it", "You can do it", "Nice pace, hold it",
"Last minute, give it everything".

Rendered offline with Piper TTS (MIT, rhasspy/piper 2023.11.14-2) and the
free en_US-ryan-medium voice (v1.0.0 phoneme map). Not listened to by ear;
levels were normalized with ffmpeg loudnorm (I=-16, TP=-1.5).

If a clip fails to load, the lab falls back to the browser's speechSynthesis.
