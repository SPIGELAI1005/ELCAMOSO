Acoustic piano and choir loops in this pack
(loop-acoustic-piano, loop-acoustic-piano-melodic, loop-choir, loop-choir-melodic)
are original ELCAMOSO arrangements (C# minor, 120 BPM) rendered from the
shared Adaptive Music sprites already in the app:

  public/sample-packs/recorded/music-v1/piano.m4a
  public/sample-packs/recorded/music-v1/choir.m4a

Those sprites were generated from the FluidR3_GM soundfont (MIT License),
Copyright (c) Frank Wen and contributors. FluidR3_GM is used unchanged as
a sample source; the musical lines, voicing, hall/room treatment and loops
are original to ELCAMOSO Viral Epic Party and are not Midnight Boost audio files.

Midnight Boost (fusion-midnight-boost) itself was not modified. Its choir
is the same music-v1 "choir" sprite, played by the darkrave Auto-DJ genre
(stacked chord tones, hall reverb ~2.8 s, mix 0.36, entering at intensity
> 0.6). Viral Epic Party reuses that sample and the stacked-chord + hall approach,
voiced SATB and arranged to the accordion hook.

Other Viral Epic Party stems (stadium beat, accordion, chant, kit, etc.) are
original synthesis / Piper TTS and do not use FluidR3_GM.
